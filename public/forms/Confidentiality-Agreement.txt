- Organizational business strategies and operations

- Trade secrets and business methods
- Marketing and business development plans
- Any information marked as "Confidential"

CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS
================================================================================

I understand and agree that:

1. PROTECTED INFORMATION
   I will not disclose patient or organizational information to any unauthorized 
   person, except as required by law or proper authorization.

2. HIPAA COMPLIANCE
   I will comply with all Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act 
   (HIPAA) regulations regarding Protected Health Information (PHI).

3. NEED-TO-KNOW BASIS
   I will access and use confidential information only when necessary to 
   perform my job duties.

4. SECURE HANDLING
   I will maintain confidential information in secure locations and use 
   appropriate safeguards.

5. NO UNAUTHORIZED DISCLOSURE
   I will not discuss, copy, or transmit confidential information to:
   - Unauthorized employees
   - Former colleagues
   - Family members or friends
   - Social media or public forums
   - Competitors or other organizations

6. WORKPLACE SECURITY
   I will follow all security procedures for passwords, access codes, and 
   system login information.

7. DOCUMENT HANDLING
   I will properly dispose of documents containing confidential information 
   through shredding or secure destruction.

8. ELECTRONIC COMMUNICATIONS
   I will not send confidential information via unsecured email, text 
   messages, or social media.

- Continues indefinitely after separation of employment
