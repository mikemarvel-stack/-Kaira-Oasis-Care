# Kaira Oasis Care LLC - Downloadable Forms Guide

## Overview
This document provides an overview of all available forms for download on the Kaira Oasis Care LLC website. Forms are organized by category and available for immediate download as PDF files.

## Employment Forms

### 1. Employment Application
**File:** Employment-Application.pdf
**Purpose:** Comprehensive application form for job applicants seeking positions at Kaira Oasis Care LLC
**Contains:**
- Personal information section
- Employment history (current and previous)
- Education and qualifications
- Professional references
- Acknowledgments and certifications
**Use Case:** Initial step in hiring process

### 2. Job Questionnaire
**File:** Job-Questionnaire.pdf
**Purpose:** Detailed questionnaire to assess job fitness and professional experience
**Contains:**
- Background and experience questions
- Work style and patient care approach
- Flexibility and availability
- Skills and competency assessment
- Professional development interests
**Use Case:** Supplement to application; evaluates specific competencies

### 3. Employment Verification
**File:** Employment-Verification.pdf
**Purpose:** Verification of employment history with previous employers
**Contains:**
- Previous employment dates and positions
- Job responsibilities
- Performance assessment
- Reason for separation
- Rehire eligibility
**Use Case:** Reference verification and background check

## Compliance Forms

### 4. Background Consent Form
**File:** Background-Consent-Form.pdf
**Purpose:** Authorization for comprehensive background check
**Contains:**
- Types of background checks permitted
- Disclosure and understanding of process
- Confidentiality agreement
- Results documentation area
**Use Case:** Required before background check is conducted

### 5. Central Registry Check
**File:** Central-Registry-Check.pdf
**Purpose:** Authorization for central registry criminal background check
**Contains:**
- Registry check authorization
- State selection options
- Match notification procedures
- Rights and appeal information
**Use Case:** Compliance for roles involving vulnerable populations

### 6. Drug Testing Consent
**File:** Drug-Testing-Consent.pdf
**Purpose:** Consent form for pre-employment and random drug screening
**Contains:**
- Test type authorization
- Drug-free workplace policy acknowledgment
- Understanding of testing procedures
- Medical history disclosure
- Results tracking
**Use Case:** Pre-employment screening and ongoing compliance

### 7. Code of Conduct Agreement
**File:** Code-of-Conduct-Agreement.pdf
**Purpose:** Employee agreement to company policies and ethical standards
**Contains:**
- Core values and ethical standards
- Conduct expectations (patient care, professional conduct, safety)
- Confidentiality and privacy requirements
- Substance abuse policy
- Harassment and discrimination policy
- Safety and health standards
- Consequences of violations
**Use Case:** Employment condition; signed at hire

### 8. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure Agreement
**File:** Confidentiality-Agreement.pdf
**Purpose:** Comprehensive confidentiality and HIPAA compliance agreement
**Contains:**
- Definition of confidential information
- HIPAA compliance requirements
- Secure handling procedures
- Electronic communication guidelines
- Exceptions to confidentiality
- Legal consequences
**Use Case:** Employment condition; renewal recommended annually

## Form Filing & Storage

### Digital Files
- All forms are stored in `/public/forms/` directory
- Forms are text-based files convertible to PDF
- File naming: Hyphenated lowercase (e.g., Employment-Application.txt)

### Printing & Fillable Options
- Forms can be printed for manual completion
- Digital completion recommended for easier processing
- All forms have signature lines for authorization

## Compliance & Legal

### HIPAA Compliance
All employment and confidentiality forms comply with:
- Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)
- State employment laws
- Local regulations in Kentucky, Indiana, Ohio, and Tennessee

### Regular Updates
Forms are reviewed and updated:
- Annually to reflect policy changes
- When regulations change
- Based on internal process improvements

## Form Usage Guidelines

### For HR Department
1. Maintain complete records of all signed forms
2. Store confidential forms securely
3. Use consistent filing system
4. Review compliance on quarterly basis
5. Update templates as needed

### For Managers
1. Ensure all new hires complete required forms
2. Verify completeness before submission to HR
3. Communicate policies to employees
4. Follow up on outstanding forms

### For Employees
1. Complete forms accurately and completely
2. Ask HR for clarification if needed
3. Keep copies for personal records
4. Update information when circumstances change
5. Understand confidentiality and legal implications
